Sudoku and Cake is a single-player Sudoku game that runs entirely on the device. There is no account, no login, no in-app purchase, no subscription, no advertising, no user-generated content and no network code of any kind: no URLSession, no sockets, no third-party SDKs. Please test it with Airplane Mode on; every feature works the same. There are no credentials because there is nothing to sign in to. Tapping any difficulty on the home screen starts a puzzle immediately.

1. SCREEN RECORDING
Attached to our reply in App Review. Recorded on a physical iPhone in Airplane Mode, starting from launch. It includes the app's only permission prompt (notifications, for the optional streak reminder).

2. DEVICES TESTED
iPhone 17 Pro Max, iOS 27.0 (physical device).
Also tested on iPhone and iPad simulators on every change, including the largest accessibility text size.

3. WHAT IT DOES AND FOR WHOM
A Sudoku game for people who already play on paper or in another app. Puzzles are generated on the device and rated by the solving technique they need, not by how many digits are missing, so difficulty is consistent and every puzzle is solvable by logic alone. Hints teach: the first names the technique, the second shows where to look, and only the third fills the cell. Also includes a daily puzzle with streaks and a calendar, stats and achievements, pencil marks, undo/redo, manual puzzle entry, share-by-link, a Home Screen widget, and full VoiceOver and Dynamic Type support. It solves a common problem: free Sudoku apps usually need ads, a connection and an account. This one has no server, so it needs none of them. Rated 4+.

4. HOW TO REACH EACH FEATURE
No setup, no sample files.
- Play: tap Gentle, Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert on the home screen.
- Pencil marks, Hint, Undo/Redo: the control bar under the board. Tap Hint repeatedly to see all three levels.
- Daily puzzle: Daily puzzle on the home screen; the calendar icon opens past days.
- Streak reminder (only permission prompt): on the Daily screen, switch on the reminder toggle. Declining leaves the app fully usable.
- Stats and achievements: Stats on the home screen.
- Enter a puzzle by hand: Import a puzzle on the home screen.
- Share: the Share button on the game screen. The link encodes the puzzle itself; no server is involved.
- Widget: long-press the Home Screen and add the Sudoku and Cake widget.
- Settings: Settings on the home screen.
- Spanish: set the device or app language to Spanish.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. No data providers, authentication, payments, AI services, analytics, crash reporting or ads. The hint engine is a deterministic solver built into the app. Only Apple frameworks are used, plus our own SudokuKit package. The single entitlement is an App Group, used only so the widget can read a small status file that the app writes. All data stays on the device.

6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in every region and nothing is geo-gated. It ships in English and Spanish and follows the device language. The daily puzzle is based on the UTC date, so everyone gets the same puzzle on the same day.

7. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Neither applies. There is no gambling, wagering, virtual currency or chance mechanic. Sudoku is public domain, and every puzzle is generated on the device. The icon and sounds are original, generated by scripts in our repository. No licensed fonts, images, audio or data sets.
